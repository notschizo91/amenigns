🧩 Assembly Fit

The name's outline is cut into the letter's face, enlarged by the fit gap on every side, but the bottom of the letter stays solid (back wall) — press the name into the pocket, it seats against the wall, glue it. ~0.15–0.3 mm gap fits snugly on most printers. Back wall 0 cuts all the way through like a stencil.

🎨 Appearance & Heights

Both pieces print flat on the plate. The name seats on the back wall, so it stands proud by its thickness minus the pocket depth (letter − back wall) — the classic layered look.
